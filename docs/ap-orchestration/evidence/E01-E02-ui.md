The policy editor now supports an explicit recovery rule: jurisdiction, configured noncash tax receivable, allowed rates or evidenced stated amounts, price basis, fraction, dates, independent review group and rounding. New fields and decisions begin blank; an incomplete rule is invalid. Policy proposal and activation remain separate.

A recoverable line can carry exact source citations for original base, tax, gross, date, jurisdiction, price basis and printed rate where applicable. Amounts are entered in USD, while each source citation explicitly distinguishes printed dollars from cents. Citations retain exact source revision/hash, complete page, global text offsets, quote and raw value. Review displays cost and tax receivable separately. Tax-only supplier credits do not require an unrelated cost correction plan.

Payment details display the immutable recognition-bound accounting scope and hashes; they offer no new department selector. External plan review exposes returned location, class, project, customer and billability references without fabricating mappings.

The cost correction UI now preserves exact store/department/project coordinates in source-share reachability and signed capacity checks. Reclassification selects an authenticated current cost position rather than silently dropping dimensions. This uses the existing cost-state query; no endpoint or backend authority was added.
