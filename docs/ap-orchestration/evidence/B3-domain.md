## Implemented surface

- Immutable versioned company policy: authenticated subject groups, threshold bands, ordered quorum, escalation deadlines, bounded delegations and revocation. A controller proposes; an independent human activates. Later policy activation uses the previous governance group. Original case/proposal creator cannot vote. A subject or delegated principal cannot fill two positions. All retained votes are revalidated before recognition and payment authority.
- Configurable supplier identity, aliases, SKU/unit/price catalog and immutable destination revisions. Trusted E.164 phone contact requires retained evidence independent of an invoice or credit note. Agent proposal, human callback attestation and different human approval are separate. Bank changes must use the previously approved contact. `simulated` is allowed only in local mode; `operator_recorded` is an evidence-backed operator attestation, not a claim this software made a call.
- PO/receipt and non-PO contract/service-acceptance line support. Source/line SKU, units, price and quantities must agree. Accepted quantities may be partial and must explain exact accepted net and tax cents; omitted lines, unsupported reductions and automatic short-payment are denied. Persistent consumption tracks PO/contract and receipt/service capacity across invoices and source revisions. Legacy B1 consumption is preserved.
- Atomic approved bill and recognition journal. A 7,700-cent original explicitly accepting one of two 3,850-cent units records original=7,700, approved=3,850, disputed=3,850, cash=0, outstanding=3,850. No bank command is created. The legacy bill approval API cannot bypass active B3 governance. Recertifying unchanged economics uses the existing journal and never creates a second recognition.
- Linked supplier credit/return recognition names the original approved allocation. Cumulative net, tax and returned units cannot exceed the accepted original. Nonrecoverable tax reverses in original cost proportions; tax-only adjustments are explicit. Each credit reverses remaining original allocation cents, avoiding independent rounding drift. Freight and discount reversals are separately bounded.
- Explicit human credit-only application consumes an available same-supplier linked credit against an approved unreserved bill, advances gross paid amount, retains an immutable application receipt, and creates no bank command or additional journal. Source/business recognition remains separate from settlement.
- Read projection separates original, accepted, applied credit, supplier credit still available, cash settlement and remaining obligation. Credit cases expose their recognition journal and original bill links. Historical source/candidate values are never rewritten to impersonate accepted values.
- Authoritative QBO hook: `loadRecognizedBusinessExport(ctx, scope, caseId)` in `convex/lib/apSystem/businessExport.ts`, exposed by human-only `apBusinessExports:current`. It reads original approved revision/plan and checks exact posted journal (including dimensions). Returned signed cost allocations total the recognition amount; negative discount allocation must be treated explicitly by the downstream QBO adapter.

## API map

`src/ap-system/domain-contracts.ts` supplies typed Zod schemas for policy, supplier proposal/verification, acceptance, credit, delegation, domain request/vote and detail projection. `apBusinessConfig` exposes `proposePolicy`, `activatePolicy`, `registerTrustedContact`, `proposeSupplier`, `verifySupplier`, `approveSupplier`, `delegate`, `revokeDelegation`, `view`. `apBusiness` exposes `propose`, `vote`, `applyCredits`, `proposals`.

Agent proposal calls use existing scoped agent credentials; human decisions use Convex authenticated identity and configured active human seat. No browser `actor`, `approved`, or callback proof boolean can grant authority. Proposal request binds exact case revision/snapshot, policy version and supplier revision; vote binds exact proposal ID/hash. Replay conflicts include actor and payload.

Root payment seam: `resolveAPBusinessPaymentAuthority(ctx, scope, billIds)` returns `null` only for all legacy/B1 bills without a B3 binding, otherwise `{beneficiary:{vendor,externalAccountId,version}}`. B3 missing/stale policy, supplier, review or approval denies; mixed legacy/B3 or different beneficiary denies. Root must call at request, approval and first dispatch, preserving possible-send recovery identity. Governance invalidation cancels only provably unattempted reservations; attempted operations stay reserved.

## Explicit holds, handoffs and remaining roadmap

- USD enabled. Other original currencies are preserved with `unsupported_currency`, not mislabeled USD. Recoverable tax/tax-receivable posting, department/project dimensions, unknown store/accounts and unsupported periods receive owned holds. Expense/inventory/fixed-asset/prepayment accounts use the configured chart. This is synthetic company policy and not tax advice or practitioner approval.
- Replacing a supplier's previously held verification contact is held pending a separate governance procedure; invoice-supplied replacement contact cannot self-authorize a bank change.
- Changing a posted amount or cost allocation requires linked credit/correction; recertification cannot rewrite recognized economics. Original B1 bills can be recertified only when the proposed B3 allocations exactly match the original journal.
- Root C3 payment integration, D2 browser/API workflow and A/QBO full/partial/credit settlement sync are separate integration dependencies. This contribution does not claim those end-to-end flows accepted. `apAccountingExport.ts`, payment engine/demo schema, server/UI, shared capability/practitioner docs and canonical registry were not edited.
- B4 next, after independent B3 acceptance: supplier statement matching and exceptions; AP-subledger/GL reconciliation; accrual proposal/approval/recognition/reversal; locked periods, unpaid carryforward; controlled reopen and separate adjustment/reversal history. Preserve recognition/payment/bank reconciliation separation throughout.
