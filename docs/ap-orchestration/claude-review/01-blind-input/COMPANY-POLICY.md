# Declared synthetic company policy

Policy provenance: the Golden State input facts' accountingPolicy and company/sweetgreen/profile.ts, the first-wave AP policy contract, and the separately configured business/close/tax scenario policies. This is a compiled policy statement by the implementation team, not an independently signed company handbook. Any discrepancy between original documents and this declaration is a review finding. The reviewer should challenge policy completeness and economic meaning rather than assume implementation is correct. Source revision for this handoff: integration 7eaad4438b816b51a95faa4d622d5e715f5aaf6b; actual older run revisions are disclosed only in phase 2.

## Entity, currency and accounts

The entity is the synthetic Sweetgreen simulation, Hayes Valley store (sf-hayes-valley); it is not actual Sweetgreen accounting. Currency and this delivery are USD only. Other currencies and FX are deferred. All numeric scenario amounts below use integer cents unless a dollar sign is printed. Account 1000 is operating cash, 1180 a separately approved synthetic recoverable-tax receivable, 1200 inventory, 1300 prepayment, 1500 fixed asset, 2000 trade AP, 2010 accrued liabilities, and 5000 food/operating expense. Accounts 1010/1020 are other configured cash accounts when opening evidence refers to them. Account existence does not itself justify a classification or entitlement.

The first-wave Golden State policy treats the supplied produce as immediately consumed restaurant supplies, expense account 5000, invoice month 2026-10, store sf-hayes-valley. First-wave department and project are null. Supplier identity and unit prices must match the trusted catalog: KALE-24/case/3850, ROM-CS/case/4275, AVO-48/case/6890, TOM-25/case/5420. Contract validity and terms are printed in the original. Zero price variance is permitted without separate governed resolution. Bank instructions are sourced from an independently verified supplier master, never from invoice text.

## Evidence and authority

Complete original pages and supported invoice facts are required. An unresolved highlight location requires a recorded review of the correct original; it does not turn unsupported content into evidence. PO evidence and independently accepted goods receipt must cover the selected goods and units. Non-PO service acceptance is permitted only under an explicitly adopted contract/service policy. Unknown supplier, SKU, unit, contradictory facts or missing receipt require owned resolution. A later source revision retains earlier evidence and invalidates affected authority. Repeated filenames, source IDs or parser rows do not create a new economic obligation or physical receipt capacity.

Field review, business acceptance/recognition, and exact payment authorization are separate decisions. An AP/controller agent can propose but not impersonate a human decision. Required stages must be current at use time; aliases of a principal/seat cannot provide independent votes. New source, supplier, policy, amount, destination or date must be checked against the authority snapshot. Only provably unsent requests may be canceled/replaced without reconciling an earlier possible send.

## Expanded business policy (only when a case explicitly adopts it)

Explicit accepted quantities and cost allocations may be lower than invoiced quantities; preserve the rest for dispute/cutoff review. Permitted cost treatments are expense, inventory, prepayment and fixed asset with explicit evidence. Generic component examples use an owner stage (one current independent principal), and amounts above 5000 cents also require a finance stage; variance/resolution requires finance. The I2/C03 run deliberately uses owner quorum two followed by finance quorum one. Supplier verifier and supplier approver are separate roles. Payments still require separate exact authorization. Whether these roles are sufficient should be challenged by the reviewer.

Supplier credits link to the original approved bill and exact original/current cost/tax allocation, with receiving evidence for returned units or an explicitly supported price/tax correction. Capacity is cumulative across credits, including renamed copies. Credit recognition and subsequent allocation to a bill are distinct operations. Preserve original journals; corrections are append-only and link the current allocation snapshot. Signed freight/discount legs and store/department/project coordinates remain explicit. Cash discharge is the selected amount less selected recognized credit; zero-cash application uses its separate governed operation.

## Timing, recovery and close

Payment initiation is eligible at 00:00 UTC on the selected date, not a promised arrival date. An uncertain bank response is not a failed payment or verified return. Preserve the original immutable command, destination and idempotency key; reconcile the original provider identity and authoritative posted bank event. Never infer settlement solely from a request/acceptance. A verified return and separately authorized replacement must retain their links. Duplicate event delivery or polling is not new economic activity.

Use separately reviewed accounting dates, original dates and period allocations. Received obligations outside invoice recognition require explicit accrual or evidenced non-obligation/timing disposition at close. Review current remaining physical quantities, not merely the historical existence of an accrual. Receipt rows for the same physical units cannot replenish capacity when split, merged, renamed or reordered. A partial merged row requires explicit physical shares.

Supplier statement comparison, AP/GL comparison, cash/bank reconciliation and reviewed openings are separate requirements. Equal totals can conceal omitted offsetting items. A difference requires investigation/evidenced disposition, not an invented balancing entry. Unpaid recognized AP may carry forward. Period locks, explicit reopening and linked reversal/adjustment preserve history. FakeBank liquidity is not evidence of opening ledger cash. Review the appropriateness of these policy assumptions and any unprovided cutoff evidence.

## Opt-in tax and dimensions

Recoverability is never inferred from invoice tax. A synthetic approved policy must specify jurisdiction, effective dates, exclusive/inclusive or stated-amount basis, permitted rate(s), recovery fraction, account 1180 and independent tax-review stage. C15-C18 declare SYNTHETIC-JURISDICTION, tax point 2026-10-19, validity 2026-01-01 through 2026-12-31, 10% rate where stated, and explicit recovery fraction. This does not establish real US tax eligibility. Require independent eligibility evidence separate from invoice bytes. Calculated tax is half-up per line; acceptance/recovery use original proportions; successive credit allocation uses cumulative half-up proportions and preserves final remaining cents. C16 explicitly includes freight and subtracts discount from taxable base, with taxable-base proportion for partial acceptance. Per-line original and accepted header shares must be supported.

Later recovery changes apply to remaining attributable tax under a newly approved policy, with exact original line/cost/tax snapshot, target and bucket movement, separate current financial/variance/tax approval, and an open accounting period. Historical entries remain immutable. No general authorization for tax filings, refunds, compound tax, withholding or reverse charge is declared.

Cost dimensions require approved store/department/project catalogs and exact shares. With cost_allocations scope, department/project are cost attributes only. The separate cost_and_settlement policy requires one homogeneous approved department (or consistent explicit null) and carries it to AP and cash. Store and project are not invented for cash. Corrections preserve original coordinates.

## Trusted contact and external replication

A replacement trusted contact requires independently acquired evidence with origin/time, distinct current recorder/verifier/final approver and escalation when the old channel is unavailable. A renamed copy of an invoice is not independent verification. An unresolved change holds new authority. A possible existing send must retain the original destination/identity. Approving a new contact is not approving new bank details; supplier revision and case recertification are separate.

QBO US sandbox is an accounting replica through Accounting API/OAuth2. Increase sandbox is the payment execution provider; QBO must not initiate merchant payment processing. Replication needs exact account/vendor mappings and approved source/preview identity; store maps explicitly to Class, department to Location only when supported, and project/customer/billability requires current entitlement. Unsupported representations are held. Lost responses require original-identity lookup/readback. Retired authority cannot be replaced to create a fresh operation; scoped historical read-only recovery may investigate the original receipt. Do not treat local mocks as external acceptance.
